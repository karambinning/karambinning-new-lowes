01 · About

A neighbourhood Chinese takeaway on Lynn Road.

Hot food, paper bags, a phone that rings on a Friday night — a small Chinese takeaway tucked into the Gaywood end of Lynn Road, serving locals from PE30 and the surrounding streets of King’s Lynn.

New Lowes — sometimes listed as Lowe’s House on the delivery sites — is the kind of corner-shop Chinese the neighbourhood has on speed-dial: chow mein, curry sauce, crispy aromatic, and the rice that goes with everything.

Phone orders welcome. Collection from the shop. Delivery to nearby streets where the round permits.
